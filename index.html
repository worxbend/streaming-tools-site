A few good
questions.

Do I need two machines?

No. The OBS control and monitoring tools also work with OBS on the same computer. Connect to 127.0.0.1:4455. The chat clients connect directly to Twitch or YouTube.

Which tool should I start with?

Choose SceneDeck for a Linux desktop remote, obsctl-rs for a terminal command center, or obs-stats to watch stream health. Every tool works independently; you do not need all seven.

Does msm start my stream?

Preparing your Twitch and YouTube broadcasts does not automatically start OBS. You choose when to go live. Current versions of msm also offer optional OBS controls, including starting and stopping your stream.

Is everything free and open source?

Yes. All seven tools are MIT licensed and developed in the open on GitHub. You can inspect the source, read an installer before running it, or download a prebuilt release.
